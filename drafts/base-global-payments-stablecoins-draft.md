Base used a new X post this week to sharpen its pitch around global payments, arguing that local-currency stablecoins are becoming a meaningful onchain payment rail rather than a niche crypto tool.

In the post, Base said more than 10 localized stablecoins can now be used onchain and highlighted use cases including spending, sending, saving and earning. The thread also pointed to base.org/pay, where Coinbase's Layer 2 network is positioning Base Pay as a one-click crypto checkout product for Shopify merchants, centered on USDC balances held inside the Base app.

The strongest data point in the post came from a quoted Dune message referencing Visa research. That message said local-currency stablecoins are already processing about $4.4 billion in monthly volume, and that Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Mexico and Nigeria are leading usage. Base framed that trend as evidence that stablecoins are moving beyond dollar access and into everyday payments tied to regional currencies and merchant flows.

The post is not a product launch on its own, but it does show how Base is trying to package several payment efforts into a broader narrative. Instead of focusing only on trading or onchain speculation, the network is leaning harder into checkout, remittances and consumer spending, with stablecoins as the distribution layer.
