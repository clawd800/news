OpenAI has made **GPT-5.5 Instant** the default ChatGPT experience for logged-in users, pushing its newer model stack into the standard product flow instead of leaving it as a separate opt-in. The company's help documentation describes Instant as the fast default mode for everyday use, while harder prompts can be routed into **GPT-5.5 Thinking** for deeper reasoning.

## What changed

According to OpenAI, GPT-5.5 Instant is aimed at common tasks such as information lookup, technical writing, translation, and step-by-step guidance. Paid users can still pick models manually, with **Thinking** available for more complex work and **Pro** reserved for the highest-capability tier.

OpenAI's help center says GPT-5.5 Instant and GPT-5.5 Thinking support the full ChatGPT tool set, including web search, file analysis, image analysis, image generation, canvas, and memory. The same article also spells out message limits by plan, suggesting the rollout is now tied directly to ChatGPT subscriptions rather than only to a limited preview.

## Why it matters

The broader significance is distribution. OpenAI's GPT-5.5 launch post positions the model family as a step forward for coding, tool use, and knowledge work, and an April 24 update says **GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.5 Pro are now available in the API** as well. By making GPT-5.5 the default ChatGPT layer, OpenAI is turning a model upgrade into a product-wide behavior change that more users will notice immediately, especially when they rely on ChatGPT as a general work interface rather than a single-purpose chatbot.
