tZERO has signed a definitive agreement to combine with **FG Imperii Acquisition Corp.**, a Nasdaq-listed special purpose acquisition company, in a proposed transaction that would take the tokenization infrastructure provider public.

The companies announced the agreement on November 2. It assigns tZERO a **$350 million pre-money equity valuation**, including up to $25 million contingent on specified milestones. That figure is a valuation, not the amount of new cash being raised.

The combined company could receive up to approximately $231 million from FG Imperii's trust account, before transaction expenses and subject to public shareholders redeeming their shares. Existing tZERO shareholders are expected to roll all their equity interests into the combined business.

## Listing targeted for early 2027

The companies expect the transaction to close in early 2027 and the combined company's common stock to trade on NYSE American under **TZRO**. Closing still requires shareholder approvals, applicable regulatory consents and filings, an effective Form S-4 registration statement, and exchange listing approval.

tZERO provides infrastructure for issuing, trading, settling and holding tokenized assets through its technology platform and regulated subsidiaries. Its institutional business supplies capabilities through APIs and white-label services, allowing partners to offer digital-market products without building the entire underlying stack themselves.

The company says transaction proceeds would strengthen its balance sheet, expand technology and regulated infrastructure, and support commercial growth and new products. CEO Alan Konevsky and the existing management team are expected to remain in charge.

For tokenization infrastructure, the concrete development is a signed route toward public-market financing. Neither the listing nor the maximum cash proceeds are secured yet.
