Otter.ai has added **13 transcription languages** to its AI meeting assistant, expanding beyond English, French, Spanish, and Japanese. The company also announced 14 further languages and an upcoming mode that follows speakers as they switch languages within one meeting.

The distinction matters: **31 languages is the eventual total, not the number available today**, and automatic language switching is still described as coming soon.

## Available now

The additions are Mandarin Chinese (Simplified), German, Italian, Cantonese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, Malay, Dutch, Turkish, Vietnamese, and Polish. Otter says they are available across desktop, mobile, and browser at no additional cost across its plans. Users currently select a supported language in settings.

According to the company, support extends beyond transcripts to summaries, action items, speaker identification, AI Chat, and search. Its Work Mode can draw on previous conversations before a meeting and produce follow-up emails and CRM updates afterward.

Otter says its speech models are built and operated in-house. TechCrunch reports that the company used publicly available training datasets and native-speaking volunteers to review transcripts; the announcement does not establish comparable accuracy across every new language.

## Still to come

The next group includes Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Hebrew, and ten other languages. Separately, the planned multilingual option is intended to detect language changes without manual adjustments. Otter has not specified a launch date for either in its announcement.

For international teams, the immediate change is broader language coverage. Following a mixed-language discussion automatically remains a promised capability, not a feature to assume is already live.
