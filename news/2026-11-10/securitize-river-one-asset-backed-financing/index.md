Securitize announced on November 9 that it signed a definitive agreement to acquire River Credit Labs, known as River One, an institutional asset-backed financing platform. The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. Financial terms were not disclosed.

The proposed acquisition would extend Securitize's business beyond issuing and servicing tokenized assets into lending against financial assets. River One is developing technology intended to connect stablecoin capital with traditional lending markets; the announcement does not describe a completed integration or a newly available lending product.

## Financing loan portfolios

Founded in New York in 2025, River One specializes in warehouse financing backed by income-generating assets, including small-business loans and receivables. These facilities give lenders capital to fund loans as they originate them.

According to Securitize, River One's platform combines origination, structuring, facility setup, borrowing-base reporting, collateral monitoring and funding operations. It was incubated by Framework Ventures and received backing from Framework Ventures and Maple Finance.

The companies aim to let institutions bring financial assets onchain and obtain financing against them through an integrated platform. River One's planned approach retains conventional underwriting and legal protections, including collateral requirements, portfolio monitoring and special-purpose vehicles designed to be bankruptcy-remote.

## Agreement, not deployment

Securitize also referenced the acquisition in its third-quarter results announcement. The practical development is a planned expansion into credit infrastructure, rather than evidence that stablecoin-funded lending has already scaled. Closing the transaction, integrating the technology and establishing relationships with capital providers and borrowers remain execution steps ahead.
