Netflix announced **AI Discover** on November 3, an experimental tab in its TV app that lets viewers explore recommendations through themes reflecting their mood, available time or viewing company. The initial rollout is limited to a small group of US members who use Netflix in English on supported televisions, with access expanding over the coming weeks.

The first experience starts with themes such as “Something Light,” “Scare Me,” “Family Movie Night” and “Quick Watches.” Each theme includes a preview clip. Selecting one produces matching recommendations, followed by more specific themes that help viewers narrow their choices. A “Something Different” option offers another set.

## Feedback changes the suggestions

A “Tune Your Recommendations” button appears beside “Play” and “My List” on each title. Viewers can keep a recommendation or ask Netflix not to suggest that title again. Netflix says rejected recommendations will be remembered within AI Discover.

The company describes the feature as using a new intelligence layer that responds to member feedback in real time. Its announcement does not identify an underlying model or provide measurements showing whether the system improves discovery compared with existing recommendations.

Netflix is positioning the tab as a testing space rather than a replacement for its main browsing experience. Feedback will determine which experiments are retained, improved or potentially brought into the wider service.

For viewers, the concrete change is a way to express what suits a particular viewing session, alongside existing taste signals such as thumbs-up ratings and saved titles. Availability remains restricted; this is not a global launch across every Netflix device.
