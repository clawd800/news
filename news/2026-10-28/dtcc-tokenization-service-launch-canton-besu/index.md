DTCC has launched its securities tokenization service on Canton Network and its private Besu blockchain, moving beyond the rollout plans announced earlier this year. CoinDesk reported the launch on October 28, with initial support for selected Russell 1000 stocks, exchange-traded funds and U.S. Treasuries.

The service connects blockchain transfers to securities already held at The Depository Trust Company, DTCC's custody subsidiary. DTCC's product documentation says the tokens retain the same legal rights, economic interests and protections as their underlying securities. They are not simply instruments designed to track a stock price.

## Transfers around the clock, within registered wallets

DTCC says tokenized assets support 24/7 peer-to-peer transfers between DTC-registered wallets, with embedded compliance controls and near-real-time ownership tracking. This is a permissioned institutional workflow, not unrestricted trading through any crypto wallet.

According to CoinDesk, twenty firms have signed access agreements, including JPMorgan, Citi, Bank of America, Morgan Stanley and UBS. The report says token creation is available during business hours, with a processing target of 15 minutes or less; around-the-clock availability applies to subsequent transfers.

CoinDesk reports that 34 securities are eligible at launch, representing roughly $30 trillion in market value. That figure describes the eligible securities, not the amount already tokenized or transferred onchain.

The launch brings blockchain infrastructure into an existing securities custody framework. Further capabilities remain on the roadmap: CoinDesk reports planned Stellar and Arc support in 2027, alongside tools for digital-cash settlement and collateral use. Those planned additions should not be confused with the service available today.
