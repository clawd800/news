Brazilian exchange operator **B3 and six financial institutions are developing Open BRL**, a stablecoin linked to the Brazilian real, alongside shared infrastructure for transferring funds and settling digital-asset transactions.

The participants are Bradesco, BTG Pactual, Itaú Unibanco, Safra, Santander and XP. In an interview published by Valor on November 27, B3 products vice president Luiz Masagão said the platform is expected to begin operating in the **first quarter of 2027**. That is a planned start date, not confirmation that the token is already available.

## Issuance and technology split

Banco B3 will initially handle issuance and maintain the backing reserves, while B3 will operate the technology. The exchange and its partners intend to contribute resources and use the currency in their digital-economy projects.

Other financial institutions will be able to join from the start of operations, according to Masagão. He described the objective as opening the initiative to the broader Brazilian financial market, rather than limiting it to the founding participants.

The initial operating structure may also change. Masagão said the banks could consider a separate, fully independent operation as the project expands; that remains a possibility rather than an established arrangement.

For digital-asset infrastructure, the concrete development is a proposed common settlement instrument backed by a group of established financial firms. The next milestone is moving from that development plan to an operating platform. Neither the target launch window nor the participation list, by itself, establishes adoption or transaction volume.
