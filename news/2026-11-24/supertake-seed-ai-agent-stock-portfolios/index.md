Supertake has raised **$20 million in seed funding** for a service that uses AI agents to turn investment ideas into stock portfolios, TechCrunch reported on November 23. Adjacent led the round, with participation from Coinbase Ventures and Union Square Ventures.

Users describe a view about how markets or industries will change. The system works with them to refine that view into an investable “take,” rather than requiring them to select individual stocks. According to the founders' interview, Supertake currently works with Coinbase and Robinhood, combining frontier AI models with proprietary software that constrains agent behavior.

## Trading within defined limits

The team says its controls include trade-size limits, asset allow and deny lists, portfolio drift limits and monitoring for anomalous activity. Agents cannot see brokerage credentials, and deposits or withdrawals require explicit user consent. These are company-described safeguards, not evidence of independently verified investment performance.

The current portfolios consist of stock picks. Derivatives, short positions and prediction-market transactions are potential future expansions, not capabilities established by the announcement. CEO Lincoln Murr described the product as an evolution of robo-advisers rather than a prediction market.

Access remains limited: Supertake's website advertises an invite-only waitlist, while offering a route to skip it by connecting a funded brokerage. It also displays a leaderboard of users' investment themes.

For agentic finance, the concrete development is a funded product moving beyond market commentary into brokerage-linked portfolio management. Its announced controls concern trading authority and account access; they do not establish that AI-selected portfolios outperform alternatives.
