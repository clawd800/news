Hut 8 has commercialized the first phase of its **Beacon Point** campus in **Nueces County, Texas** through a **15-year lease** covering **352 megawatts of IT capacity**, with **base-term contract value of $9.8 billion**. According to Hut 8's May 6 announcement, the tenant is an undisclosed **high-investment-grade company** that will use dedicated infrastructure for **AI training and inference**.

## What changed

Hut 8 said the deal turns a site originally positioned for bitcoin-mining demand into its second major AI data center campus. The company said Beacon Point has an interconnection agreement for **1,000 MW** of utility capacity, with **initial energization expected in Q1 2027** and the **first data hall** targeted for delivery in **Q3 2027**.

The company also said the lease includes **three five-year renewal options** that could raise total contract value to about **$25.1 billion** if fully exercised. Hut 8 described the project as a **triple-net lease** and said the first phase is designed to **NVIDIA's DSX reference architecture**.

## Why it matters

The announcement is another concrete example of a listed bitcoin miner repositioning power-heavy sites toward AI infrastructure. Hut 8 said the Beacon Point deal lifts its total contracted AI data center capacity to **597 MW** and aggregate base-term contract value to about **$16.8 billion**.

That does not guarantee every future expansion gets built, and Hut 8 has not named the tenant. Still, a signed 15-year lease is a more durable milestone than a generic mining-to-AI pivot plan. CoinDesk separately reported that investors pushed Hut 8 shares sharply higher after the announcement, showing how closely the market is watching miners that can turn power access into long-term AI capacity.
