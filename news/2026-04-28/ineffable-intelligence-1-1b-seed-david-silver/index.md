London-based **Ineffable Intelligence** has launched with a **$1.1 billion seed round**, a striking amount for a company that is only weeks old and still focused on foundational research rather than a commercial product.

## What Was Announced

Law firm **Cooley**, which advised the deal, said the financing was led by **Sequoia Capital** and **Lightspeed Venture Partners** at a **$5.1 billion post-money valuation**, calling it **Europe's largest seed financing to date**. The UK government separately said its **Sovereign AI Fund** and the **British Business Bank** are co-investing in the company, adding public backing to the syndicate.

## Why It Matters

The lab was founded by **David Silver**, the former head of reinforcement learning at Google DeepMind and a professor at **University College London**. On its website, Ineffable says it wants to build a **"superlearner"** that discovers knowledge from its own experience rather than depending on human-created training data, using reinforcement learning as the core method.

That makes the company notable beyond the size of the round. The bet is not just on scaling today's language models, but on reviving the more ambitious line of work behind systems like AlphaGo and AlphaZero: agents that improve by interacting with an environment and learning from outcomes.

The conservative read is that Ineffable is still an early research lab, not a product company. But the financing shows investors remain willing to place very large bets on alternative frontier AI approaches when the founding team has a rare track record in reinforcement learning.
