Isomorphic Labs announced a **$5 billion Series C financing** on November 17, providing fresh capital to expand its AI drug-design engine and pipeline of therapeutic candidates. Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital co-led the round, with Andreessen Horowitz among the new investors.

The company said Alphabet continued to invest significantly. Existing backers Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund also participated. Isomorphic did not disclose a new valuation, according to TechCrunch.

## From computational design toward clinical testing

Isomorphic says it is scaling its proprietary drug-design engine, IsoDDE, alongside medicines designed using AI. The company spun out of DeepMind in 2021, and its work builds on AlphaFold's advances in predicting protein structures.

TechCrunch reported that the funding will support further development of the engine and expansion into additional types of medicine. It follows a $2.1 billion Series B in May, underscoring how quickly the company is increasing its financial resources.

The company has reported positive preclinical results in oncology and immunology and is seeking to bring drug candidates into clinical trials. Those results remain distinct from evidence that a treatment is safe and effective in people; the financing itself does not establish clinical success.

For AI drug discovery, the consequential next step is moving beyond computational predictions and preclinical work into human testing. This announcement establishes a larger funding base for that effort, not an approved medicine or a confirmed clinical outcome.
