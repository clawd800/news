Superhuman announced on November 19 that its **Go platform SDK is generally available** to partners and customers, alongside plans to bring Go agents into Docs and Mail. The SDK adds agent-building capabilities, while more than 20 new partner agents are scheduled to begin rolling out over the coming weeks.

The partner lineup includes Databricks, Amplitude, DeepL, Legora and Blackboard. Superhuman describes specialized tasks such as checking product-usage claims, supplying business metrics and translating writing in a user's voice.

## Document agents with an audit trail

In Superhuman Docs, users will be able to share documents with agents, mention them to delegate work and configure triggers that run without the document being open. The company says agent actions will be logged, with version history showing changes and who authorized them.

That functionality is **not broadly available today**: open beta is scheduled for December 1 for self-serve customers and the first quarter of 2027 for enterprise customers.

## Inbox integration follows in stages

In Mail, Go will replace Ask AI and connect inbox workflows to other tools. Planned capabilities include searching connected services, updating a CRM and drafting replies using meeting notes or internal documents. Mail features are scheduled for beta this month and general availability in early 2027.

A customizable personal Go assistant will begin rolling out over the next few months. Superhuman says it will suggest actions across connected tools while leaving users in control of what happens.

The immediate developer release is the SDK; the wider announcement describes a phased rollout, not a finished suite available to every customer.
