Sierra has raised **$1.75 billion in Series F funding at a $30 billion valuation**, TechCrunch reported on November 23. Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners led the round. The valuation is twice the $15 billion level reported for its previous financing in May.

The company, co-founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, builds AI agents for enterprise customer experience. TechCrunch reports that the new capital will support development of its agent technology and expansion of its sales organization.

## Revenue claims provide context

In a separate company update, Sierra said it had exceeded **$400 million in annual recurring revenue**, doubling from $200 million in five months. It also reported adding more than $150 million in ARR during the third quarter and working with almost half of Fortune 50 companies.

Those figures are company-reported. ARR is an annualized recurring-revenue measure, not a statement that Sierra has already recognized that amount in annual revenue or reached profitability.

Sierra describes its commercial model as charging for outcomes rather than tokens consumed. Its examples include a completed insurance claim, a refinanced loan or a customer retained. The company says its agents are moving beyond individual support conversations toward work spanning weeks or months and involving multiple systems.

The financing is a substantial new commitment to enterprise agents. The operating question remains whether longer-running deployments can deliver reliable, measurable outcomes at scale; a higher private valuation alone does not establish that performance.
