Nvidia is discussing credit support for Nscale's planned AI data center in West Virginia, according to an October 30 TechCrunch report citing The Information and Bloomberg. The proposed arrangement would help finance construction and electricity costs for a campus serving Anthropic. It remains reported negotiations, not an announced guarantee.

## What is already documented

Nscale's September 18 SEC filing confirms that it signed GPU-service agreements with Anthropic on August 25. Those agreements cover dedicated infrastructure at the Monarch Compute Campus for Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 GPUs and provide for aggregate payments of **up to approximately $44.6 billion**.

That figure describes potential payments under the service agreements, not construction financing already raised or revenue already earned. The filing locates Monarch in Mason County, West Virginia, on an approximately 2,250-acre site acquired through Nscale's purchase of American Intelligence & Power Corporation.

## What the proposed support would change

TechCrunch describes the discussions as involving a residual-value guarantee. Under the reported structure, Nvidia could step in following a default on construction payments, temporarily take over the facility and help find a buyer and replacement tenant.

Such support would extend Nvidia's financial involvement beyond supplying accelerators into the infrastructure needed to operate them. It could help a cloud provider obtain financing while linking the chipmaker more closely to project execution and customer demand.

The distinction matters: signed compute contracts establish a commercial commitment, but do not by themselves pay for construction. The verified development is the Anthropic service agreement; Nvidia's additional construction-related backing remains a proposal whose final terms and completion are unconfirmed.
