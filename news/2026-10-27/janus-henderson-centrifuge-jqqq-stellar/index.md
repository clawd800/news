Janus Henderson and Centrifuge announced **JQQQ** on October 27, a blockchain-native fund seeking to track the Nasdaq-100 Index under license from Nasdaq. It launches exclusively on **Stellar**, adding equity-index exposure to a tokenization market already serving Treasuries, money-market funds and credit.

Centrifuge supplies the fund's blockchain infrastructure. The issuer's legal disclosures identify JQQQ as a segregated portfolio of Anemoy Capital SPC Limited in the British Virgin Islands. Anemoy Asset Management is the investment manager, while Janus Henderson Investors US serves as sub-investment manager.

## Access is restricted

The fund is available only to eligible investors in jurisdictions where it can lawfully be offered, and **is not available to U.S. persons**. Its Nasdaq license should not be confused with an endorsement: the announcement explicitly says Nasdaq does not issue, endorse, sell or promote the product.

The structure provides indirect economic exposure through an underlying investment product tracking the index. It does not give investors direct ownership of the index's constituent companies. Tokenized shares also carry blockchain, wallet and smart-contract risks alongside investment risk.

## Composability remains conditional

The companies describe potential uses in programmable portfolios, collateral and lending. Those possibilities are not a confirmation of live integrations. The release says such functions would be supplied by independent third parties, depend on eligibility and applicable law, and fall outside Janus Henderson's management of the fund.

Janus Henderson says its broader onchain platform now includes more than $1 billion across Treasuries, structured credit and public equities. JQQQ extends that platform into another licensed equity benchmark without making the product universally accessible.
