Chainflip's website says its services are temporarily unavailable following reports that roughly **$4.86 million** in assets left the cross-chain swap protocol's Ethereum vault on November 21. The official notice directs users to its community channel for updates but does not specify a loss total or explain the cause.

Blockchain tracker Onchain Lens reported outflows of approximately **520.2 ETH, 3.25 million USDC and 758,000 USDT**, according to crypto.news. Crypto Briefing also reported the same asset amounts and said the protocol had entered Full Safe Mode, halting transactions while the team investigates.

## Signing-key compromise remains a theory

Onchain Lens said the withdrawal carried a valid signature associated with the vault's aggregate signing key. That observation raises questions about the signing process, but it does not establish how the signature was obtained or prove a particular compromise mechanism.

A valid signature and a confirmed root cause are different findings. The reported loss should therefore remain attributed to investigators until Chainflip publishes its own accounting and technical explanation.

## Funds reportedly moved rapidly

Analytics firm AMLBot said the stablecoins were converted into ETH and nearly all the proceeds entered Tornado Cash within minutes, according to crypto.news. Both reports described approximately 2,909 ETH moving through the mixer; that figure includes converted stablecoins, rather than representing an additional loss.

For users and liquidity providers, the immediate confirmed operational issue is service unavailability. The unresolved questions are the final loss amount, responsibility for affected balances, the incident's root cause and the conditions for safely restoring service.
